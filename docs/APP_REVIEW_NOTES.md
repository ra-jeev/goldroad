2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
GoldRoad is a free daily number path puzzle for players of all ages (rated 4+) who like short logic and math games. Each day there are two new puzzles. The player builds a path across a grid from the start to the flag. Each tile adds to or subtracts from the score, and the path must end exactly on the target score. It gives a short daily brain exercise of a few minutes, with streaks and medals to bring players back. There are no ads, no account and no tracking.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP
No login, demo account or sample files are needed.
- Open the app. Home shows today's Classic road.
- Tap tiles next to the footprints to build a path to the flag. Use Undo to step back, or Retry to start again.
- Reach the flag with the score equal to the target to solve. First try = gold, second = silver, third = bronze.
- Tap Hint at any time for help. There is no penalty.
- After Classic is solved, Expedition unlocks from Home.
- Past Roads lets you play earlier daily roads. Stats shows streaks and medals. Settings has the daily reminder, the community stats switch, and links to the privacy policy and support page.
- After the first daily solve, the app asks one time whether to share anonymous play stats. Nothing is sent before the player says yes.
- Add the GoldRoad widget from the Home Screen to see the streak and the countdown to the next road.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- GoldRoad server (playgoldroad.com, our own backend on Cloudflare): sends the daily puzzles, answers hint requests, and receives anonymous play stats only if the player opts in. No personal data and no account.
- Apple iCloud (iCloud key-value storage): syncs the player's own puzzle history between their devices.
- Apple local notifications: the optional daily reminder.
- Apple StoreKit: the standard App Store rating prompt.
No third-party analytics, ad, authentication, payment or AI services are used.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. All players get the same daily puzzles and the same features. The app is in English.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. GoldRoad is a puzzle game. It is not in a regulated industry and it contains no third-party protected content. All puzzles, art and text are our own.
